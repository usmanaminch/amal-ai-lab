# Day 29 — Essay Writing Trainer AI

This is an AI writing practice project.

## What it does

- AI generates a fresh essay prompt
- Student writes their own essay
- Essay limit is 2000 characters
- Student submits for grading
- AI gives score out of 100
- AI gives letter grade
- AI gives rubric breakdown
- AI gives what the student did well
- AI gives what to improve
- AI gives revision advice
- AI gives a teacher-style comment

## Files

```text
api/generate-essay-prompt.js

```

## Note

This app is a writing coach. It does not write the essay for the student.
